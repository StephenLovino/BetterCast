# BetterCast - App Store Listing Copy

## App Name
BetterCast: Display Receiver

## Subtitle
Wireless second display for Mac

## Description
Turn your iPhone or iPad into a high-performance wireless display for your Mac.

BetterCast streams your Mac screen to your iOS device with hardware-level encoding for near-zero latency at 60 FPS. Use it as a portable second monitor, a mirror for presentations, or a dedicated preview display — all without cables, dongles, or paid subscriptions.

SETUP IN SECONDS
- Install BetterCast Sender on your Mac (free download at bettercast.online)
- Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network
- Select your device from the Sender app — your Mac display appears instantly

BUILT FOR PERFORMANCE
- Hardware-accelerated H.264/HEVC encoding for minimal CPU usage
- Adaptive bitrate to match your network conditions
- Native 4K support with crystal-clear text rendering
- 60 FPS smooth playback with ultra-low latency

DESIGNED FOR REAL WORK
- Extend or mirror any Mac display
- Touch input relay for interactive control from your device
- Aspect fill mode to eliminate letterboxing
- Custom device naming for multi-device setups
- Persistent auto-reconnect to known senders

COMPLETELY FREE
No subscriptions. No ads. No watermarks. BetterCast is built to be the wireless display tool you can rely on every day, at no cost.

Requirements: macOS 13+ with BetterCast Sender installed. iOS 15+. Both devices must be on the same local network.
